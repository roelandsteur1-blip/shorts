A new YouTube channel

YouTube has no way to create a channel through its API, so this one step is by hand:

  1. Open youtube.com/create_channel while logged in with your Google account. To keep several channels under one login, choose a name of your own: that makes a Brand Account.
  2. Give it a name, picture and banner (can be done later).
  3. Come back here and continue: you'll connect it at the end.
